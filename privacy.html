Privacy — Rabbits Access Card

Rabbits Access Card is a private tool used only by Tony Vargas (Studio 410) to read and send his own Gmail and to read his own Google Calendar, Drive, Docs and Sheets. It is not offered to the public.

Data accessed through Google APIs stays on Tony's own computer, is used only to produce his personal briefings and emails, and is never sold, shared, or transferred to third parties.

Rabbits Access Card's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
